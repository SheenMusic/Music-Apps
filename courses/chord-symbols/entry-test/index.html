入学自测Entry Self-Test

这份自测帮助你判断自己是否适合学习和弦记号用法。第一部分是 10 道音程题，第二部分是和弦记号题。

每题答完会马上告诉你对错和讲解。不会的题可以跳过，跳过不得分。测试结束后会生成一份成绩单，报名时可以发给老师。

This self-test helps you find out whether Chord Symbols is right for you. Part 1 has 10 interval questions; Part 2 covers chord symbols.

You will see whether each answer is correct, with an explanation, straight away. You can skip any question you are unsure of, but skipped questions score zero. At the end you will get a results report that you can send to the teacher when you enrol.
