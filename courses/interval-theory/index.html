音程理论训练课Interval Theory

用大约一周，把音程这件事真正理清楚。Build a clear understanding of intervals in about a week.

音程是音乐理论中非常基础的一环，也是之后学习和弦、即兴伴奏、扒谱与编曲的重要基础。但“基础”并不代表它一定简单：不少已经学习音乐很久的人，依然容易混淆自然音程、变化音程、复音程和转位等概念。Intervals are a foundation of music theory and of later work with chords, improvised accompaniment, transcription, and arranging. Yet foundational does not always mean easy: even experienced learners can find diatonic intervals, chromatic intervals, compound intervals, and inversion confusing.

这些知识其实不需要花很长时间反复学习。课程把音程理论拆成六个短单元，按照建议的节奏学习，大约一周就可以把主要知识点完整梳理一遍。You do not need to spend a long time going over these ideas repeatedly. The course breaks interval theory into six short units. At the suggested pace, you can work through the main concepts in about a week.

这里的“一周”是课程设计的学习周期，而不是学习效果的承诺。按时完成练习、及时解决没有理解的问题，比单纯把课程“看完”更重要。One week is the intended study schedule, not a guarantee of results. Completing the exercises regularly and asking about anything you do not understand matter more than simply finishing the material.

课程内容Course Content

  1. 01　认识音程01 · Understanding intervals

    分清度数与音数，认识旋律音程和和声音程，建立音程最基本的概念。Distinguish an interval’s number from its size in tones and semitones. Learn about melodic and harmonic intervals and establish the basic concepts.

  2. 02　音程的性质02 · Interval qualities

    学习大、小、纯、增、减音程，理解自然音程与三全音。Learn major, minor, perfect, augmented, and diminished intervals, and understand diatonic intervals and the tritone.

  3. 03　变化音程与等音程03 · Chromatic and enharmonic intervals

    理解自然音程、变化音程和等音程之间的区别，解决“声音距离相同，为什么名称却不同”等常见问题。Understand the differences between diatonic, chromatic, and enharmonic intervals, including why intervals can sound the same yet have different names.

  4. 04　单音程与复音程04 · Simple and compound intervals

    把超过八度的音程与对应的单音程联系起来，学习识别、换算与构成。Relate intervals larger than an octave to their corresponding simple intervals. Practise identifying, reducing, and building them.

  5. 05　音程转位05 · Interval inversion

    理解音程转位以后度数与性质发生的变化，并把前面的知识联系起来。Understand how an interval’s number and quality change when it is inverted, and connect this with the earlier concepts.

  6. 06　快速识别与综合复习06 · Faster recognition and review

    利用自然音程、五线谱位形、转位等规律，逐渐减少机械计算，更快地判断音程。Use patterns in diatonic intervals, shapes on the staff, and inversion to rely less on mechanical counting and identify intervals more quickly.

学习方式How You’ll Learn

这不是把传统乐理课录成视频让你从头看到尾。The course is not a series of recordings of traditional theory classes to watch from beginning to end.

课程以互动网页、谱例和练习为主。每个单元只处理有限的知识点，完成学习后立即通过练习检查理解；系统能够完成的判断交给系统，需要进一步解释的问题则可以向我提出。You will learn through interactive web pages, musical examples, and exercises. Each unit covers a small set of ideas, followed by exercises to check your understanding. The system checks answers where it can, and you can ask me about anything that needs further explanation.

建议按照课程安排的节奏持续完成，而不是一次性浏览所有内容。Work steadily at the suggested pace rather than browsing all the material in one sitting.

适合谁Who It’s For

学习这门课需要具备最基本的五线谱识读能力：至少能够知道五线谱上的音符分别是什么音。不要求已经掌握完整的乐理体系。You need basic staff-reading skills: at a minimum, you should be able to identify the notes on a staff. You do not need a comprehensive knowledge of music theory.

尤其适合：This course is especially useful if you:

  • 正在学习即兴伴奏，希望补齐基础乐理的人Are learning improvised accompaniment and want to strengthen your theory foundations.
  • 想学习扒谱，但对音程关系还不够熟悉的人Want to transcribe music but are not yet confident with interval relationships.
  • 准备进一步学习和弦、编曲等内容的人Are preparing to study chords, arranging, or related topics.
  • 学过音程，但变化音程、复音程、转位等概念仍然容易混淆的人Have studied intervals but still find chromatic intervals, compound intervals, or inversion confusing.

对于即兴伴奏而言，音程不是一个孤立的理论章节，而是之后理解和弦结构、旋律与和声关系的一项基本能力。For improvised accompaniment, intervals are more than a separate theory topic. They are essential to understanding chord structures and the relationship between melody and harmony.

完成课程后After the Course

课程的目标不是让你背下一套音程定义，而是建立一个相对完整的音程理论框架。The aim is to build a coherent framework for interval theory, rather than memorise a set of definitions.

完成六个单元后，你应该能够更有条理地判断音程的度数与性质，理解自然音程、变化音程、复音程与转位之间的关系，并为之后的即兴伴奏、扒谱、和弦与编曲学习建立基础。After the six units, you should be able to identify interval numbers and qualities more systematically and understand the relationships between diatonic intervals, chromatic intervals, compound intervals, and inversion. This provides a foundation for further study of improvised accompaniment, transcription, chords, and arranging.
