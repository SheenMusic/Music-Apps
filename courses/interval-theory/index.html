音程理论训练课Interval Theory

用一周时间，系统梳理音程理论。Systematically study interval theory in a one-week learning cycle.

音程是理解和弦、即兴伴奏、扒谱与编曲的重要基础。但即使学过多年音乐，也可能仍然容易混淆自然音程、变化音程、复音程和转位等概念。Intervals are an important foundation for understanding chords, improvised accompaniment, transcription, and arranging. Even experienced musicians may still find diatonic intervals, chromatic intervals, compound intervals, and inversion confusing.

课程将音程理论拆分为六个短单元，通过互动学习、谱例与配套练习，逐步建立较为完整的知识框架。建议每天完成一个单元；一周是标准学习周期，而非固定的学习进度或效果承诺。The course divides interval theory into six short units, using interactive learning, musical examples, and exercises to build a coherent framework. One unit per day is the suggested pace; one week is the standard study cycle, not a fixed schedule or a guarantee of results.

课程内容Course Content

  1. 01　认识音程01 · Understanding intervals

    分清度数与音数，认识旋律音程和和声音程，建立音程最基本的概念。Distinguish an interval’s number from its size in tones and semitones. Learn about melodic and harmonic intervals and establish the basic concepts.

  2. 02　音程的性质02 · Interval qualities

    学习大、小、纯、增、减音程，理解自然音程与三全音。Learn major, minor, perfect, augmented, and diminished intervals, and understand diatonic intervals and the tritone.

  3. 03　变化音程与等音程03 · Chromatic and enharmonic intervals

    理解自然音程、变化音程和等音程之间的区别，解决“声音距离相同，为什么名称却不同”等常见问题。Understand the differences between diatonic, chromatic, and enharmonic intervals, including why intervals can sound the same yet have different names.

  4. 04　单音程与复音程04 · Simple and compound intervals

    把超过八度的音程与对应的单音程联系起来，理解识别、换算与构成规律。Relate intervals larger than an octave to their corresponding simple intervals. Practise identifying, reducing, and building them.

  5. 05　音程转位05 · Interval inversion

    理解音程转位以后度数与性质发生的变化，并把前面的知识联系起来。Understand how an interval’s number and quality change when it is inverted, and connect this with the earlier concepts.

  6. 06　快速识别与综合复习06 · Faster recognition and review

    利用自然音程、五线谱位形、转位等规律，逐渐减少机械计算，更快地判断音程。Use patterns in diatonic intervals, shapes on the staff, and inversion to rely less on mechanical counting and identify intervals more quickly.

学习方式How You’ll Learn

课程以互动网页应用、谱例和随堂练习为主，学员通过点击操作与测验主动学习理论知识，并使用配套应用完成课后练习。Learn actively through interactive web applications, musical examples, and in-lesson exercises, then use the companion application to complete follow-up practice.

每个单元结束后，需要提交系统生成的练习成绩单。教师将根据练习结果与实际表现进行评估；通过后，方可进入下一单元。After each unit, submit the results report generated by the practice system. The teacher reviews the results and overall performance; you may proceed to the next unit once approved.

建议每天完成一个单元，但可根据自己的时间和学习情况调整进度。遇到问题时，可以向教师提出与本课程内容相关的问题。One unit per day is recommended, but you can adjust your pace to suit your schedule and learning needs. You may ask the teacher questions related to this course.

适合谁Who It’s For

学习本课程需要具备基本的五线谱识读能力，能够辨认五线谱上的音符；不要求已经掌握完整的乐理体系。如果还不能独立识谱，建议先补充基础识谱知识，也可以向教师咨询学习路径。You need basic staff-reading skills and should be able to identify notes on the staff. A comprehensive knowledge of music theory is not required. If you cannot yet read notes independently, study the basics first or ask the teacher about possible next steps.

尤其适合：This course is especially useful if you:

  • 正在学习即兴伴奏，希望补齐基础乐理的人Are learning improvised accompaniment and want to strengthen your theory foundations.
  • 想学习扒谱，但对音程关系还不够熟悉的人Want to transcribe music but are not yet confident with interval relationships.
  • 准备进一步学习和弦、编曲等内容的人Are preparing to study chords, arranging, or related topics.
  • 学过音程，但变化音程、复音程、转位等概念仍然容易混淆的人Have studied intervals but still find chromatic intervals, compound intervals, or inversion confusing.

学习目标Learning Goals

完成六个单元后，你应当能够更有条理地判断音程的度数与性质，理解自然音程、变化音程、复音程与转位之间的关系，为后续的音程听力训练、和弦理论与即兴伴奏学习建立基础。After the six units, you should be able to identify interval numbers and qualities more systematically and understand the relationships between diatonic intervals, chromatic intervals, compound intervals, and inversion. This provides a foundation for interval ear training, chord theory, and improvised accompaniment.
