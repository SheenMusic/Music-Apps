你可以围绕一个具体能力进行短期、集中的专题训练，也可以从自己的基础和目标出发，进行持续的一对一学习。Build a specific skill through a short, focused course, or study one-to-one over time, starting from your experience and goals.

专题训练Focused Training

围绕一个具体能力进行短期、集中的训练。每个单元大概需要一个星期的学习时间，可以独立学习，也可以根据需要进行组合。Short, structured courses focused on a specific skill. Each course takes roughly a week, and you can take courses individually or combine them to suit your needs.
