音程听力训练课Interval Ear Training

用 1～2 周，开始建立音程与实际声音之间的联系。Start connecting intervals with their sound over one to two weeks.

音程听力是扒谱、即兴伴奏和编曲中非常基础的一项能力。很多人能够认识五线谱、理解音程与和弦，却仍然很难从实际声音中判断音高的运动与距离。Hearing intervals is a foundational skill for transcription, improvised accompaniment, and arranging. Many learners can read music and understand intervals and chords, yet still find it difficult to hear the direction and distance between pitches.

这门课从最基本的声音方向与距离开始，通过聆听、比较、模唱和构唱，逐步建立不同音程的声音印象。相比单纯记住音程名称，更重要的是让耳朵真正参与到音乐判断中。Starting with pitch direction and distance, you will listen, compare, imitate pitches, and sing intervals from a reference note. These exercises gradually build a sense of how different intervals sound. The priority is to involve your ears in musical decisions, rather than just remember interval names.

听觉能力需要通过重复练习逐渐建立，因此课程设置为 1～2 周的训练周期。完成全部单元并不意味着听觉训练结束，而是帮助你建立一套之后可以继续使用的训练方法和基础能力。Aural skills develop through repeated practice, so the course is designed for one to two weeks of training. Completing the units is not the end of ear training; it gives you foundational skills and a practice method you can continue to use.

课程内容Course Content

  1. 基础听觉判断Pitch direction and distance

    从声音向上还是向下、距离更宽还是更窄开始，建立最基本的音高运动与距离感。Begin by hearing whether pitches move up or down and whether the distance is wider or narrower. Develop a basic sense of pitch movement and distance.

  2. 单音与八度Single notes and octaves

    通过模唱建立对单个音高的控制，并练习不同八度位置之间的关系。Imitate individual pitches to develop control, then practise the relationships between notes in different octaves.

  3. 二度与七度Seconds and sevenths

    通过构唱和双音模唱，逐渐建立二度与七度的声音印象。Sing intervals from a reference note and imitate the two notes of a harmonic interval to build a sense of seconds and sevenths.

  4. 三度与六度Thirds and sixths

    继续训练大小三度、大小六度，感受不同音程之间的声音差异。Practise major and minor thirds and sixths, and explore how these intervals differ in sound.

  5. 四度与五度Fourths and fifths

    练习纯四度、纯五度以及三全音相关的声音关系。Practise perfect fourths, perfect fifths, and the pitch relationships of the tritone.

  6. 复音程Compound intervals

    把训练范围扩展到超过八度的音程，继续练习构唱和双音模唱。Extend your practice to intervals larger than an octave, continuing to sing intervals from a reference note and imitate pairs of notes.

整个课程共 18 个训练单元，由基础的声音判断逐步进入具体音程训练。The course contains 18 training units, progressing from basic listening skills to specific intervals.

学习方式How You’ll Learn

这门课不是以“听一个音程，然后猜它叫什么”为主要训练方式。The main approach is not to hear an interval and guess its name.

你需要真正参与声音的产生：听到参考音以后唱出指定的另一个音，或者听到同时发声的两个音以后，分别把低音与高音唱出来。You will actively produce the sounds: sing a target note after hearing a reference pitch, or listen to two simultaneous notes and sing the lower and upper notes separately.

练习过程中可以通过实时音准反馈观察自己的演唱结果，再针对不稳定的部分反复训练。学习期间遇到无法判断的问题，也可以向我提出。Real-time pitch feedback helps you check your singing and practise the parts that are not yet consistent. You can also ask me about anything you are unsure of during the course.

适合谁Who It’s For

学习这门课需要具备基本的五线谱识读能力，能够认识五线谱上的基本音高即可。You need basic staff-reading skills: being able to identify pitches on a staff is enough.

不要求先完成“音程理论训练课”，也不要求已经具有成熟的音程听辨能力。You do not need to complete Interval Theory first, or already be confident at recognising intervals by ear.

尤其适合：This course is especially useful if you:

  • 想学习扒谱，但经常面对声音不知道从哪里开始判断的人Want to transcribe music but often do not know where to begin when listening.
  • 学习即兴伴奏，希望加强旋律与和声听觉的人Are learning improvised accompaniment and want to strengthen your ear for melody and harmony.
  • 学习编曲，希望建立更直接的声音判断能力的人Are learning to arrange and want to make musical decisions more directly by ear.
  • 已经学过不少乐理，但实际听觉能力没有同步建立的人Have studied music theory but feel your aural skills have not developed alongside it.

对于扒谱而言，音程听力尤其重要。认识一个音程解决的是“它是什么”，而听觉训练解决的是：当这个声音真正出现时，你能不能发现它。Hearing intervals is especially important for transcription. Theory tells you what an interval is; ear training helps you recognise it when you actually hear it.

完成课程后After the Course

这门课的目标不是让你在 1～2 周以后立刻听出所有音程，而是建立音程听觉训练的基本能力和方法。The aim is to establish basic skills and methods for interval ear training, not to have you recognise every interval after one or two weeks.

完成 18 个训练单元后，你应该能够更有意识地感受声音运动的方向和距离，并开始具备从参考音构唱指定音程、从双音中分别听取两个声音的能力。After the 18 units, you should have a more conscious sense of pitch direction and distance, and begin to be able to sing a specified interval from a reference note and hear the two notes of a harmonic interval separately.

这些能力也可以继续用于之后的扒谱、即兴伴奏和编曲训练。You can continue to use these skills in transcription, improvised accompaniment, and arranging.
