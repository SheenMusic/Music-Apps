一对一音乐课Private Lessons

每个人学音乐的起点和目标都不太一样。Everyone comes to music with a different starting point and different goals.

有的人想学会自己给一首歌伴奏，有的人希望能听懂音乐里的和声和旋律，也有人带着自己的作品来，和我一起看看还能怎么修改。You might want to accompany a song yourself, understand the harmony and melody you hear, or bring in your own music and work with me on how to develop it.

在一对一的课程里，我会从你现在会什么、想学什么开始，再一起安排接下来的学习内容。In private lessons, we start with what you can already do and what you want to learn, then plan the next steps together.

课程主要围绕Lessons focus on 键盘即兴伴奏、音乐理论、听力与扒谱、编曲与创作improvised keyboard accompaniment, music theory, ear training and transcription, arranging and composition展开。如果你正在准备音乐相关的升学或作品集，也可以带着具体的问题来找我。. If you are preparing a music school application or portfolio, you are welcome to bring specific questions to our lessons.
