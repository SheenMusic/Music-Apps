過不去

Guò Bú Qù

Voice & Piano Transcription · 3:45

Voice · Piano

Original studio recording from 也許明天 · A-Mei (張惠妹)

A complete voice-and-piano transcription of A-Mei’s original studio recording of Guò Bú Qù, from the album 也許明天. The piano accompaniment becomes particularly dense during the song’s more emotionally intense vocal passages, making individual notes and inner voices challenging to distinguish by ear.

The transcription aims to reconstruct these piano textures as faithfully as possible while translating the recording’s flexible pulse and expressive timing into clear, readable rhythmic notation.
