Haru Haru

Voice & Piano Transcription · 1:23

Voice · Piano

Live performance on You Hee-yeol’s Sketchbook (KBS) · Baek A-yeon (백아연)

A complete transcription of Baek A-yeon’s shortened live rendition of BIGBANG’s Haru Haru, performed on KBS’s You Hee-yeol’s Sketchbook. Her interpretation presents the original electronic K-pop song as a compact, lyrical voice-and-piano performance.

The transcription focuses on presenting this stripped-down version in a clear, concise score, including the accurate placement and alignment of Korean lyrics within the vocal notation.
