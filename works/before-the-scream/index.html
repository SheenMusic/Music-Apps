Before the Scream

Horror · Cinematic Music · 2026 · 1:11

Violin I · Violin II · Viola · Cello · Double Bass

为 Jump Scare 前逐渐累积的紧张感而创作的弦乐作品。作品弱化明确旋律与调性，通过音色、音域、不协和音程与声响密度的变化建立不稳定的音响空间，并运用 Artificial Harmonics、Sul ponticello、Tritone、Minor 2nd 以及局部 Controlled Aleatory，使心理压力随时间逐渐增加。A string piece composed to build tension before a jump scare. Clear melody and tonality are downplayed; changes in timbre, register, dissonant intervals and sound density create an unstable sonic space. Artificial harmonics, sul ponticello, tritones, minor seconds and passages of controlled aleatory gradually increase psychological tension.
