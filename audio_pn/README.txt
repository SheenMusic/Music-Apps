Changes made for this site (2026-10-29):
- Taken from the 44.1 kHz / 16-bit set, velocity layer 10 of 16.
- 17 notes, C2-C6, every minor third: C, D#, F#, A (MIDI multiples of 3).
  The player (MT-Chord/shared/chord-stage.js) uses the nearest sample and
  shifts pitch by at most one semitone.
- Leading silence removed, cut to 6 s with a 1.8 s fade-out, gain x1.7.
- Encoded as stereo MP3, 44.1 kHz, 192 kbps (LAME header, so browsers skip
  the encoder delay).
- Filenames: sharps use s (Ds4 = D#4, Fs5 = F#5).

Replaced the earlier set (TimGM6mb soundfont rendered with FluidSynth).

2026-11-10: C6.mp3 re-encoded with a 400 Hz high-pass filter (two
2-pole stages). About half of its tail energy was low-frequency noise
below 200 Hz, which made B5 and C6 sound like heavy reverb. Other
samples have little energy there and were not changed.
